The AID tier manifest must classify each in-scope AID file or source group as one of:

- upload-content candidate
- evidence-only authority
- accepted limitation
- conflict
- recheck
- excluded with reason

Default treatment:

- `~/AID/arch/Home/`, `~/AID/FAQE/Home/`, and source-backed `~/AID/llm-reference/` are
  upload-content candidates only when evidence supports use.
- `~/AID/DOCK/Home/`, `~/AID/faq/Home/`, semantic coverage, source-stabilization notes,
  review reports, and `manifest.json` are evidence-only authority by default.
- `source_limitation`, legacy unavailable attachment, export artifact limitation, and
  intentionally excluded low-information content remain limitations.
